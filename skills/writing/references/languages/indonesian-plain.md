# Indonesian for Public Readers

Read for public-facing Indonesian: UI copy, public-service notices, guides, forms, and help content. For developer-facing Indonesian, use `indonesian.md`.

> Adapted from [bahasa-indonesia-plain](https://gist.github.com/orepras/3e1ca8974fc51d11825f142fa6a66635) by [orepras](https://github.com/orepras), revision `0221be6` (11 November 2026). See `CREDITS.md` in the source repository for permission status and changes.

## Audience and Voice

- Address the reader as "kamu" or "Anda" and keep one choice across the product. Use "kami" for the organization.
- Use "dia", "ia", or "beliau" when the gender is unknown. Do not write "dia (laki-laki/perempuan)".
- Chat may use "aku/kamu" when a pronoun is needed. Avoid forced slang: gue, lo, nih, deh.
- Use imperatives for steps the reader performs. Use descriptive sentences with a subject for what the service does.
- Warm is fine. Cute is not. Do not use humor that depends on a stereotype.

## Inclusive Wording

- Write "pengguna dengan disabilitas", not "penyandang cacat" or "difabel", unless the community uses that term for itself.
- Avoid stigma words: gila, stres berat, bodoh, lambat, lemah, cacat, abnormal.
- Do not write "keluarga normal" or assume one family structure.

## Plain Word Choices

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| manfaatkan, optimalkan | gunakan |
| fasilitasi, berdayakan | bantu |
| berkolaborasi, bersinergi | bekerja sama |
| hadirkan, deliver | buat, sediakan |
| terkait hal tersebut, berkenaan dengan | soal, tentang |
| dalam rangka, guna | supaya, agar, untuk |
| memulai proses | mulai |
| menyelesaikan tahapan | selesai |
| tangani, combat | atasi, perbaiki |
| berdampak | berpengaruh, menyebabkan |

Avoid metaphors and clichés: ekosistem, hub, portal, robust, highlight, leverage, deep dive, sinergi, akselerasi, transformasi digital. Keep them only when they name something specific.

## Sentence Patterns

- Prefer active voice and a clear subject: "Kamu bisa mengubah kata sandi kapan saja." Not "Perubahan kata sandi dapat dilakukan kapan saja oleh kamu."
- Keep sentences to 12 to 20 words, never more than 25.
- Keep paragraphs short. One topic per paragraph.
- Avoid structures translated word for word from English: "Pastikan bahwa semua kolom yang bersifat wajib telah diisi terlebih dahulu" becomes "Isi semua kolom yang wajib diisi."
- Use "-lah" and "-kah" sparingly. They make public copy sound formal or stiff when overused.

## EYD and Capitalization

- Use sentence case for headings, buttons, labels, and table headers. Capitalize proper nouns, official institution names, and the first word of a sentence.
- Capitalize a product or feature name only when it is the official name. Write "daftar ke platform ini" for the generic action.
- Do not use capitals for emphasis.
- Write "misalnya", "yaitu", "dan sebagainya" instead of "mis.", "e.g.", "i.e.", "dll.", or "etc.".
- Write "dan" instead of "&" unless it is part of an official brand name.

## Punctuation

- End every complete sentence with a period, including a list item that is a full sentence.
- Use a comma to separate clauses. Do not join two sentences with a comma.
- Use an exclamation mark only for warm success or celebration, and never more than one.
- Use an em dash for an aside. Do not use a hyphen in its place.
- Use quotation marks for direct quotes and document titles. Do not use italics for titles.
- Avoid semicolons in public copy. Two short sentences are clearer.

## Numbers

- Write the percent sign directly after the number: 50%, not 50 %.
- Write Rp directly before the amount: Rp75.000, not Rp 75.000.
- Use a dot for thousands and a comma for decimals: 1.500.000, not 1,500,000.
- Write large amounts in full words: Rp5 miliar, not Rp5M or Rp5.000.000.000.
- Use "hingga" or "sampai" for ranges: "10 hingga 20 orang", not "10-20 orang".
- Add a comparison when the amount is too large to picture easily.

## Dates and Times

- Write "4 Juni 2026". No comma, no ordinal suffix.
- Write a range as "4 sampai 8 Juni 2026".
- Write time as "10.00" or "10.00–11.30". Use a dot, not a colon.
- Write "tengah hari" and "tengah malam" instead of 12.00 siang or 12.00 malam.
- State the time zone when the audience spans several: "10.00 WIB".

## Local and Regional Context

- For regional services, add a local-language note or version for critical instructions and emergency information.
- Do not assume a fast connection, a new phone, a bank account, or an urban address.
- Use examples and names that reflect Indonesia's diversity, not only Jakarta or Java.
- Avoid references that depend on one religion, holiday, or local custom when the audience is mixed.

## Self-Check

- Can a first-time reader understand each sentence?
- Is the actor named in every rule?
- Is the pronoun choice consistent?
- Are EYD, number, and date formats consistent?
- Is any sentence a word-for-word translation from English?
- Are currency and percentage examples unambiguous?
- Is the text safe for a reader in a difficult situation?
- Did shortening remove a subject or a connector?
