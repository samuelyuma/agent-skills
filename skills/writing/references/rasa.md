# RASA Quality Check

Use after drafting public-facing content and before delivery. RASA is a lens for judging a draft, not a structure for the document. The name comes from the Indonesian word rasa, meaning taste, feeling, or meaning. The original terms are Ritmis, Akurat, Sarat makna, and Alami.

Run the four checks in order. Each has its own lane.

## R: Rhythmic

Read the text aloud. Check:

- Cadence: does sentence length vary, or does every sentence carry the same weight?
- Breathing room: are there natural pauses at the right places?
- Sound: are there harsh clusters, awkward repetition, or phrases that are hard to say?
- Flow: does the text carry the reader, or does something make them stumble?

Poor rhythm forces a reread, and a reread means the first read failed.

## A: Accurate

Check every word:

- Precision: does the word carry the meaning, or only come close?
- Connotation: does it bring a judgment or tone you did not intend?
- Filler: remove "quite", "some", "various", "might possibly".
- Grammar and sense: correct in structure and in meaning.
- One right word beats three approximate ones.

## S: Substantive

Ask what the copy carries:

- Does the line carry the whole point?
- Does the call to action give a real reason to act?
- Does the microcopy build trust, or fill space?
- Is any of it long but empty?

## A: Authentic

Ask whether a person is speaking:

- Would someone actually say this, or does it sound like a template that survived legal review?
- Does the tone fit the moment: warm when warmth helps, direct when directness helps?
- Is the brand voice consistent without feeling forced?
- Does it read as natural language, or as a translation?

Formal text can still sound human. Authentic does not mean casual.

## What Failure Looks Like

| Dimension | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Rhythmic | Every sentence has the same length and weight | Vary sentence length; read aloud and cut stumbles |
| Accurate | "Innovative solution" instead of the actual mechanism | Name the mechanism or cut the claim |
| Substantive | A call to action that says "Submit" with no reason or object | Name the action and the outcome |
| Authentic | "In today's fast-paced digital era..." | Start with the reader's situation and a concrete fact |

## Order

1. Read aloud for Rhythmic problems before going word by word.
2. Check every word for Accuracy.
3. Ask what the text is really trying to say for Substance.
4. Read again as an ordinary reader for Authenticity.

If all four pass, the text is ready. RASA does not replace the language self-check. It catches quality problems that spelling and grammar checks miss.
